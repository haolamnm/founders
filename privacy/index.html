Privacy

This site collects nothing.

For The Founders Automation is our private tool for publishing to our own YouTube channel. Only the channel owner uses it.

  • youtube.upload: upload our videos and thumbnails.
  • youtube.force-ssl: add captions and edit our video details.
  • youtube.readonly: confirm our uploads.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: read our channel stats.

The sign-in token stays on the owner's computer. No Google data is shared, sold, used for ads or used to train AI. Use follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use.

Uses YouTube API Services: YouTube Terms, Google Privacy Policy. Revoke access at Google account permissions.
